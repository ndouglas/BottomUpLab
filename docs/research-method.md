# Research method

SugarScape studies published models to determine what scientific knowledge their results support. The agreed sequence is faithful reproduction, transparent charitable reconstruction where needed, and scientific evaluation through responses, follow-ups, critiques and counterfactuals. Scientific evaluation is the highest purpose; reproducing an executable result supplies evidence for that purpose.

## Faithful reproduction

Recover the original paper, equations, code, data, parameters, random generators, update order, stopping rules and reported statistics as far as the available sources allow. Preserve distinctions between the prose, mathematical model and supplied implementation. A code match establishes the named computation; it does not automatically establish every statement in the paper.

Keep original source identities, resolved settings, fixed seeds, raw outputs, successful comparisons and discrepancies. Distinguish an implementation defect from an ambiguity, a missing input, a measurement mismatch or a limitation of the intended algorithm. Report scope precisely: original-code docking, source-equivalent reproduction, conditional reconstruction and an author-inspired variant are different achievements.

## Charitable reconstruction

When ambiguity or a plausible omission prevents a fair test, specify the strongest defensible interpretation consistent with the stated scientific question. Explain which original passage or implementation supports it, what changes, and what remains uncertain. Preserve the faithful reconstruction alongside every charitable variant.

Choose interpretations by source justification before examining whether they produce the desired outcome. Altering a rule to obtain an attractive result defines a new model or counterfactual; it does not silently repair the original. Successful charitable reconstruction narrows a discrepancy without erasing it or upgrading unrelated claims.

## Scientific evaluation

Evaluate the reproducible phenomenon, its proposed mechanism, its robustness, its applicability and its practical significance separately. Read the authors' responses and subsequent work alongside independent critiques. Give each argument its strongest defensible interpretation, preserve conflicting evidence and design tests that distinguish competing explanations.

Ask whether the proposed mechanism is necessary or sufficient for the measured result, whether a simpler alternative produces it, and which parameters or information conditions the conclusion depends on. Keep implementation correctness separate from the behavior of an imperfect learner. A correct simulation can faithfully reproduce an algorithm's mistakes.

Evaluate scientific value claim by claim. A weakened explanation may leave a valuable reproducible phenomenon or a more precise finding about algorithm design. A counterexample outside a paper's scope does not automatically refute its original claim. Likewise, a compatible simulation does not establish causal identification, equilibrium, biological calibration or real-world generality.

## Evidence and reporting

Use the [reproducibility register](reproducibility.md) for stable claim IDs, dates, evidence scope, unresolved work and links to authoritative artifacts. The [paper catalog](papers.md) tracks source families and follow-ups. Keep a dated study's original observations distinct from later analyses and extensions.

For every substantive claim, record the original source/version and prediction, the corresponding model and statistic, faithful and charitable results where relevant, the response or critique being tested, the observed evidence, the interpretation that survives, and the next unresolved question. Status belongs to the specific claim: supported, qualified, contradicted within a stated scope, or unresolved.

Fix empirical designs and judgment rules before new measurements. Retain every planned key, unsuccessful or censored run, unavailable statistic and material source limitation. Label exploratory and retrospective checks. Preserve previous artifacts and separate observation-only diagnostics from behavior-changing interventions.

## Writing scientific accounts

Build a write-up around the original question, source identity, reproduced result, competing explanations, discriminating tests and warranted conclusion. Cite primary sources and compare contributions with existing literature before making a novelty claim. Include methodological boundaries with the claims they constrain.

Working notes and publication-ready findings are different deliverables. Eight exploratory seeds, a conditional model or a successful code match can motivate further work without establishing a general theorem or external validity. Publishing, contacting authors and running a new empirical campaign remain distinct actions governed by the user's current instructions.

This method records the user's standing research objective agreed on 2026-10-10. It guides future work and does not itself authorize new experiments, tuning, publication or alteration of frozen scientific identities.
