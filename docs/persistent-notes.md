# Paid persistent notes

Two frozen collections on **2026-10-15** produced identical complete reports for the registered finite model. Retaining a paid note improves the supplied ReadAdaptive Reader routine, but this does not establish that producing it benefits the original Writer or the group. This is a local working research account, not publication or a claim about real incidents.

The [registered protocol](superpowers/protocols/2026-10-14-paid-persistent-notes.md) and [approved design](superpowers/specs/2026-10-14-paid-persistent-notes-design.md) fix the model and judgment rules. Two goods, one hidden equiprobable request, supplied Writer/Reader roles, four supplied Reader routines, four arms and 144 cost/reward strata give 4,608 ordered episodes per collection. The Reader has at most six opportunities; the Writer departs after four. Rewards settle to the original cohort, including the departed Writer. Note encoding and routines are supplied; communication was not learned.

## Measured findings

All 4,608 typed traces per report, private views, costs, original-cohort ledgers, 2,304 request means, 4,608 request contrasts, 2,304 mean contrasts, 576 envelopes and every tie agree with the independently derived source-blind reference. Native saved validation and a separate source-blind saved-data checker passed. The checker reconstructed counters and canonical hashes from retained bytes; it did not call the collector, rerun a reference census or optimize policies.

For the **fixed ReadAdaptive routine**, Retained minus Erased increases Reader expected net by **2–6 credits in all 144 strata**. Retained ReadAdaptive is correct on both requests in all strata. Erased ReadAdaptive has correctness 1 in 80 strata and 1/2 in 64. BlindGuess, InspectFirst and privileged Oracle each have exactly zero Retained–Erased contrast across all strata. Thus note persistence matters for the supplied routine that reads and uses it; correctness and cost savings are separate outcomes.

This gain is a counterfactual difference, not a claim about absolute welfare. Under Retained ReadAdaptive, the original Writer has negative expected net in **132 strata** and positive net in 12 (range **−12 to 2**). Group expected net is negative in **90**, zero in 18 and positive in 36 (range **−6 to 3**). Reader net ranges from 0 to 7. The living Reader's information benefit does not automatically compensate the departed Writer's acquisition/writing costs.

The registered **offline Reader envelope** chooses among BlindGuess, InspectFirst and ReadAdaptive using expected net before the request is known; Oracle remains a separately labeled supplied-truth control. Retained minus Erased envelope value is **0–5 credits**, positive in 120 strata and zero in 24. Across all 576 arm/stratum envelopes, InspectFirst uniquely maximizes 240, BlindGuess 192, ReadAdaptive 120; 24 retain the exact InspectFirst/ReadAdaptive tie. These are finite supplied-program comparisons, not equilibria or evidence of voluntary note production.

For example, at acquisition/write/read/inspection costs `(1,1,1,2)` and Writer reward share `0`, request-mean outcomes are:

| Retained / ReadAdaptive | 1 | 4, 5 | −4, 7 | 3 |
| Erased / ReadAdaptive | 1 | 4, 7 | −4, 5 | 1 |

The first row's whole information production costs include acquisition 1 and writing 1 paid by the Writer and reading 1 paid by the Reader, in addition to movement, handoff, pickup and delivery. A cheaper shared read alone is not the whole production cost. Erasure preserves the paid writing action; NoNote removes writing; NoAcquireNoNote also removes acquisition. These comparisons answer different questions.

## Delivery and limits

The recorded-result fixture is an exact copy of the complete saved summary. Browser delivery pools repeated metrics losslessly, preserving all ordered keys, rows, values and ties and reconstructing the complete summary for export. This is a versioned postmeasurement delivery transformation; it changes current adapter identity, not the frozen scientific source, executable or reports. The complete combined lossless browser transport is **9,641,131 bytes**, below the unchanged 16 MiB limit. Notes uses that existing bound for its 11.7 MB raw asset; every prior asset retains its 5 MiB guard. The full retained arrays include negative, wrong and zero outcomes; the displayed examples do not replace them.

Native collection feasibility does not imply lightweight browser delivery. The frozen experiments WASM was 294,029,344 bytes; the earlier postmeasurement delivery module was **317,331,040 bytes** (SHA-256 `6c854ae8eda105f17e3c02b7353daadd2c46db4ec3270e9999461ded2a1a59f0`). Earlier acceptance measured roughly 1 GB process RSS, separately from the native 512 MiB collection limit; that postmeasurement peak RSS was not separately measured. That module’s actual saved export equals the native delivery export in full. Historical full Gate/Discovery episode records were unavailable; compatibility checks of existing fixtures/source boundaries do not establish historical full-record replay.
